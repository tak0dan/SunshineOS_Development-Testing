## Cross-Platform Recovery Comparison

| Aspect               | runit                          | openrc                         |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| **Init style**     | Supervise-based, no daemon     | Init script-based, rc.d        |
| **Service dir**      | `/etc/runit/sv/`               | `/etc/init.d/`                 |
| **Runlevel dir**     | `/etc/runit/runsvdir/default/` | `/etc/runlevel/default/`       |
| **Log collection**   | `multilog` under `/var/log/runit/` | `/var/log/rc.log`           |
| **Recovery command** | `sv up/down/restart`           | `rc-service <name> restart`    |
| **Idempotency**     | `sv status` + `sv up`          | `rc-status` + `rc-service restart` |

Both models satisfy invariant **M-09** (repeatability) when recovery steps are logged with content hashes and digital signatures (per migration mandate **M-08**).
