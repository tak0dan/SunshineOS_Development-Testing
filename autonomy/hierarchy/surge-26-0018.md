## 4. Cross-Init Comparison Matrix

| Aspect | runit | openrc |
|--------|-------|--------|
| **Init binary** | `/sbin/runit-init` or `/sbin/init` (symlink) | `/sbin/init` (openrc wrapper) |
| **Service dir** | `/etc/runit/sv/<name>/` | `/etc/openrc/init.d/<name>` |
| **Boot command** | `runsvdir /etc/runit/1` | `openrc` or `service <name> start` |
| **Rollback** | `sv down <service>` | `openrc-shutdown <service>` |
| **Config source** | `/etc/runit/1/run` | `/etc/conf.d/<service>` |
| **Test ID prefix** | `TEST-BT-ARTIX-RUNIT-*` | `TEST-BT-ARTIX-OPENRC-*` |
