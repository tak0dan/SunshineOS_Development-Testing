## 6. Notes & Observations
- All runit services under `autonomy/artix/runit/recovery/` follow the `runit-run`/`supervise` pattern; each service directory contains at minimum `run`, `finish`, and `log` subcomponents.
- All openrc scripts under `autonomy/artix/openrc/recovery/` adhere to the OpenRC init script header format (`#/etc/init.d/service start|stop|restart|status`).
- Cross-system parity: filesystem mount ordering is the most divergent component between runit and openrc in Artix defaults; `runit` uses explicit service dependencies, while `openrc` relies on `rc_need` and `rc_provide` headers.
- Recovery test IDs are designed to be reusable in SunshineOS migration-engine validation pipelines (see `evolution-engine/` for integration roadmap).
- No cryptographic keys, tokens, or passwords are present in any of the above paths or evidence files.
