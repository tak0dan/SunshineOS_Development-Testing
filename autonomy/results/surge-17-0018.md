## 6. Verification & Recovery Procedures

1. **Boot into Artix emergency mode** (GRUB `e` → append `init=/bin/sh`).  
2. **runit:** `ln -sf /etc/runit/sv/<service> /etc/runit/sv/<service>_active`; execute `/usr/bin/runit-init /etc/runit/runsvdir/default`.  
3. **openrc:** `rc-update add <service> default`; regenerate initramfs via `mkinitcpio -P` (Artix kernel) or SunshineOS-equivalent.  
4. **Validate:** `sv status <service>` or `rc-status -a`; compare output against `evidence/` logs matching the test ID.  
5. **Record:** Append results to `autonomy/results/<survey>_<testid>.md`; copy raw `dmesg`/`bootlog` to `evidence/`.
